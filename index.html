ABOUT US

We Care, support and engage our communities

We are a non-profit organization whose main objective is to harness the power of our communities through care, support, and engagement with those in need. Our mission is to create a network of compassionate individuals and groups who are committed to making a tangible difference in the lives of the less fortunate. We believe in the strength of collective action and the transformative impact of community-driven initiatives. By fostering a culture of empathy and collaboration, we strive to provide essential resources, emotional support, and opportunities for personal and community growth. Through various programs and outreach efforts, we aim to address the unique challenges faced by different groups, ensuring that everyone has access to the help and support they need to thrive. Together, we can build a more inclusive and supportive society where every individual feels valued and empowered.

VISION AND MISSION

To rebuild, restore, and impact communities positively, we focus on creating sustainable solutions that address the root causes of social and economic challenges. By engaging with local leaders, stakeholders, and community members, we develop and implement programs that foster resilience, promote education, and enhance economic opportunities. Our initiatives aim to not only meet immediate needs but also to empower individuals and communities to achieve long-term stability and growth, ensuring a brighter future for all.

Areas of Focus

Outreach and Community Building

Support

Providing support to vulnerable members of the community through, Orphanages, Impoverished Schools and Old Age Homes.

Homeless

Support homeless people through Winter Projects such as soup kitchens, providing warm clothes and blankets etc

Prison

Prison visitations and engaging in inmate rehabilitation programmes, social and emotional support.

Disaster Management

Disaster Management – Assisting in humanitarian emergencies, this includes helping displaced members of community due to loss of employment, floods, fire and other external environmental factors.

Seasoned Diamonds

Engage Seasoned Diamonds in activities that make them feel heard, seen, supported and loved. This is a platform which is a hub for knowledge and wisdom sharing amongst members.

Bridge the gap

We bridge the gap for students seeking tertiary education support by securing donations and providing guidance. Our efforts connect students with financial resources, scholarships, and mentorship, making higher education accessible and attainable.

DONATIONS

As the iCare Foundation, we gratefully accept any amount of donation to help us reach the needy and the poor. Our mission is to extend care and support to those who need it most, ensuring that every donation, no matter the size, makes a meaningful impact in the lives of individuals and families facing hardships.

We are grounded in the scripture where Jesus said, "Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me" (Matthew 25:40). Inspired by these words, we strive to serve with compassion and dedication, embodying the love and kindness that Jesus taught. Your contributions enable us to continue this vital work and bring hope and assistance to those in need.
